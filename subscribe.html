Questions parents ask

Is my child's data safe?

Absolutely. The app collects no personal information, has no accounts, shows no ads, and never sends anything to a server. All artwork and progress stay in the browser on your own device. It's designed around COPPA and GDPR child-privacy principles.

Does it need internet or a subscription to keep working?

No. Drawing Master is a single file that works fully offline — on a plane, at Grandma's, anywhere. A subscription or one-time purchase supports development and unlocks the complete curriculum; the app itself never "phones home" to check your license.

What devices does it work on?

Any modern web browser — computers, tablets, and phones. It's touch-friendly, so kids can draw with a finger or stylus. Just open the downloaded HTML file.

What's included in the printable workbook?

28 A4 pages that follow the 25 lessons — tracing grids, coloring shapes, composition practice, and a graduation certificate. A great screen-free companion to the app.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If you're not happy within 30 days, contact us for a full refund — no questions asked.
